# Item art sheet prompts

39 sheets, 327 images. A sheet's prompt is its part below, then the common part on a new line (`npm run art:gen` sends both).

## Common part

```
Style: high-resolution pixel art RPG inventory icon, 32-bit era with a modern indie touch (like PlayStation / Saturn era sprites or modern HD pixel art games), fine pixel density with small pixels, about 64 to 96 pixels across each item, crisp hard-edged pixels with no blur, dark outline, rich saturated palette with smooth multi-step shading and subtle highlights that show the material (metal sheen, wood grain, leather texture, cut gems), lit from the top left, three-quarter view, bold readable silhouette, no magenta or hot pink in the item. Same orientation for every item: long items (swords, daggers, staves, wands, bows) diagonal at 45 degrees, handle at the bottom left and tip at the top right; all other items (armor, shields, tomes, rings, amulets) upright and facing the viewer. No glow, halo, aura, bloom, light rays, sparkles or particles anywhere, even on magical or holy items: show their power through material, color and gems inside the dark outline, and nothing outside the outline. Each item whole and separate, well spaced, none touching or overlapping another or the image edge. No text, names, labels or numbers anywhere. Solid flat magenta background (#FF00FF) everywhere, no gradient, no floor, no cast shadows, no frames or grid lines.
```
